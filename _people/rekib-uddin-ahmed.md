Rekib developed a computationally efficient analytical model of double-gate MOSFETs for the channel potential, subthreshold current and drain current, with an accuracy suitable for circuit simulation. He also designed, through simulation, a pore-confined phase-change memory cell that uses current crowding to reduce the programming energy.

His postdoctoral project in the group was *Compact model development for double-gate MOSFETs*.
