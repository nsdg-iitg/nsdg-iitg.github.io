{{ site.group_name }} · IIT Guwahati

Devices that harvest energy, emit light and remember.

We make and model semiconductor devices from halide perovskites, organic semiconductors and nanowires, from film growth and fabrication to defect spectroscopy, device simulation and circuits.

Our devices include flexible perovskite memristors and crossbar arrays for brain-inspired computing, lead-free and tandem perovskite solar cells, bright green perovskite LEDs, and sensors built on single nanowires. For each one we also study the physics inside: where the traps and mobile ions are, how they limit performance and lifetime, and how to measure them reliably.
