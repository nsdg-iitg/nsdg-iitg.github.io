About the simulator

  • Two-dimensional drift–diffusion model in which trap occupancy and mobile-ion density are treated as dynamical variables.
  • DC, small-signal (impedance and capacitance) and transient solvers within a single framework.
  • The internal variables of the device are accessible, so individual physical couplings can be switched off one at a time.
  • Validated against a commercial TCAD simulator.
