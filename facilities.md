<div class="facility-grid" markdown="1">

<div class="facility" markdown="1">
### Fabrication
- Mask aligner and double-sided mask writer
- Electron-beam lithography
- Laser micromachining
- Multi-source e-beam evaporator
- Thermal and organic evaporators
- RF sputtering, PECVD and RIE
- Oxidation and diffusion furnace
- Spin coaters and large-area doctor blade
- Glove boxes, wet benches, fume hoods and UV-ozone cleaner
- Wire bonder
</div>

<div class="facility" markdown="1">
### Electrical characterisation
- Deep-level transient spectroscopy (DLTS)
- Impedance spectroscopy
- I–V and C–V systems
- DC and RF probe stations
- Cryogenic chamber and cryogenic probe station
- LED solar simulator
</div>

<div class="facility" markdown="1">
### Materials and optical
- FESEM and HRTEM
- Atomic force microscopy
- Surface profilometer
- Photoluminescence and time-resolved PL
- Confocal Raman
- Contact angle goniometer
</div>

<div class="facility" markdown="1">
### Simulation
- Synopsys Sentaurus TCAD
- Silvaco TCAD
- Ansys Lumerical
- In-house drift–diffusion simulator
</div>
