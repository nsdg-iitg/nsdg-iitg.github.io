Packaging connects a chip to the outside world and protects it, and it increasingly sets the performance of the whole system. As the gains from transistor scaling slow down, more of the improvement in bandwidth and power comes from placing chips closer together, either side by side on an interposer or stacked on top of each other, joined by fine-pitch interconnects. These approaches are known as advanced packaging or heterogeneous integration.

### Current work

The group has recently begun work in this area, with a focus on packaging for flexible electronics and MEMS. Devices on flexible substrates, such as the group's perovskite memristors on PET, need interconnects and encapsulation that withstand bending and protect moisture-sensitive materials. MEMS devices need packages that protect their moving structures while still allowing them to interact with their surroundings.

Current interests include:

- Packaging and interconnects for devices on flexible substrates
- MEMS technologies and their packaging
- Integration of micro- and nano-fabrication steps into a complete process flow

The group also teaches EE640, *Introduction to Electronic Systems Packaging*, at IIT Guwahati.
