Research approach

Fabrication

Low-cost and flexible devices based on halide perovskites, organic semiconductors and other low-dimensional materials, with an emphasis on interface engineering. Machine-learning methods for predicting new device structures are being explored.

Characterisation

Carrier generation and recombination, and electronic and ionic transport, investigated with nanoscale imaging, DLTS, impedance spectroscopy and low-temperature I–V and C–V measurements.

Modelling

2D and 3D TCAD, the in-house drift–diffusion simulator CEISIM, and analytical and compact models that relate measured characteristics to physical parameters.
