Joining the group

PhD: apply through the IIT Guwahati PhD admissions to the Department of EEE or the Centre for Nanotechnology, and mention your interest in our group.

Postdocs: we host Institute and Visvesvaraya postdoctoral fellows. Write with your CV and a short research plan.

M.Tech, B.Tech and interns: projects run on devices, simulation and circuits. Write with your CV and the dates you are available.
