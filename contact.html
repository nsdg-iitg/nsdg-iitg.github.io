Positions

PhD: applications are made through the IIT Guwahati PhD admission process of the Department of EEE or the Centre for Nanotechnology. Applicants are encouraged to state their interest in the group.

Postdoctoral researchers: the group hosts Institute and Visvesvaraya postdoctoral fellows. Candidates are requested to send a CV and a brief research proposal.

M.Tech, B.Tech and internship projects: projects are offered in device fabrication, simulation and circuit design. Candidates are requested to send a CV and their period of availability.
